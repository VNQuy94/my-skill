## Lỗi người chấm ghi nhận

**with_skill**
- eval-1-survey-report-pdf: Five text colors used, including two different blues for text (navy #1E3A8A headings vs bright #1D4ED8 stat numbers/'Tóm tắt' label) and an extra orange for the single 'Lưu ý' label.
- eval-1-survey-report-pdf: Figure 1 (bar chart) is pushed to the top of page 2, separated from its section heading 'Điểm hài lòng theo hạng mục' on page 1; page 1 ends with roughly 15% empty space.
- eval-1-survey-report-pdf: The chart is a raster image (1191x500 PNG) rather than vector, so its labels use their own colors and are not searchable.
- eval-1-survey-report-pdf: Recommendations table has no timeline/owner column; 'Rà soát khung lương trong quý I' does not say which year.
- eval-1-survey-report-pdf: Only a bare page number in the footer; no document title or confidentiality marker in the running footer.
- eval-2-leave-guide-docx: Bereavement row widens the request: 'Bố đẻ, mẹ đẻ, bố vợ/chồng, mẹ vợ/chồng, vợ hoặc chồng, con mất' adds parents-in-law, which the prompt ('bố/mẹ/vợ/chồng/con mất') did not list
- eval-2-leave-guide-docx: Bảng 1 lists unpaid leave as '30 ngày/năm' and drops 'tối đa', so it reads as an entitlement rather than a cap
- eval-2-leave-guide-docx: Bảng 2 extrapolates seniority tiers up to 15-20 years (13/14/15 days); this is derived but presented as policy, not given in the request
- eval-2-leave-guide-docx: The carry-over rule is repeated four times (summary box, stat tile '31/3', list item, warning callout), which is redundant in a short guide
- eval-2-leave-guide-docx: The 'Lưu ý' content (3-day notice, carry-over) sits under heading 'Mốc thời gian cần nhớ' with no heading of its own, so the notes section is hard to find
- eval-2-leave-guide-docx: Contact email 'hr@congty.vn' sits alone in a separate Normal paragraph after a sentence ending in 'email:'
- eval-3-school-notice-nd30-pdf: The header uses many half-point sizes (12.5, 13, 13.5) next to the 14pt body. Each falls inside the ND30 range, but the size scale is fragmented.
- eval-3-school-notice-nd30-pdf: The parent agency is abbreviated to 'SỞ GD&ĐT TỈNH BẮC NINH' instead of the full 'SỞ GIÁO DỤC VÀ ĐÀO TẠO TỈNH BẮC NINH' (ND30 tolerates common abbreviations, but the full name is the safer form).
- eval-3-school-notice-nd30-pdf: Body line pitch is ~16.1pt at 14pt (single spacing). This meets the ND30 minimum, but text looks denser than B.
- eval-3-school-notice-nd30-pdf: Year range is written '2026 - 2027' with spaces around the hyphen, unlike the usual '2026-2027'.
- eval-3-school-notice-nd30-pdf: Item 5 b) speaks to parents inside 'Tổ chức thực hiện', which is meant for staff tasks, so the closing request to parents is split across two places.

## Ghi chú

- Mỗi ô chỉ có 1 lượt chạy: chênh lệch 1 tiêu chí nằm trong mức nhiễu. Cần ≥3 lượt để kết luận khác biệt nhỏ.
- Lỗi skill do lượt chạy phát hiện (venv re-exec, bullet có chữ đậm đầu dòng, tỉ lệ chữ đậm) và lỗi thiết kế do người chấm chỉ ra (hai màu xanh, hình tách khỏi tiêu đề, tự thêm nội dung, lặp ý) đã sửa SAU lượt này; cần iteration-2 để đo bản đã sửa.
